Privacy Policy

Game Buddy: Offline Games
Android package: dev.emreozturk.gamebuddy
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Game Buddy: Offline Games (GameBuddy), published under the developer name emreozturk.dev, handles information on Android. Contact: iiosturk@gmail.com.

Playing offline and information on your device

The core games work offline. The app stores your progress, scores, Buddy Coins, unlocked cosmetics and upgrades, mission and daily reward progress, favorites, game saves and settings in its private device storage. It also keeps transaction and game-run identifiers to prevent duplicate rewards and an installation identifier to reconcile progress between devices. A device-local backup or recovery copy may be retained in private app storage. Android app backup and device-transfer backup are disabled; the optional Google Play Games service provides cloud synchronization.

Age choices and family use

The app uses a neutral age-band choice to select its service behavior. The age band, including a “Prefer not to say” choice, is stored locally on the device, rather than asking for a name or exact date of birth. For users who select an age under 18, or whose age is unknown, the release disables Google advertising and Google Play Games cloud/leaderboard services. These users can play the core games and keep progress locally. Google advertising, Google Play Games and purchase services are available only in the adult 18-or-older path. Selecting an age does not verify a legal identity.

Google Play Games, cloud saves and leaderboards

In the adult service path, when Google Play Games authenticates your selected gaming profile, the app receives your player identifier and gamer display name. While signed in and online, it synchronizes your game progress and settings using Google Play Games Saved Games and submits supported game scores and total XP to Google leaderboards. Saved progress can include coin balances, earned and spent virtual currency, cosmetics, upgrades, missions, rewards, favorites, retained play history, scores and game checkpoints. The app uses a hashed player identifier to separate local saves; this remains an account-linked identifier.

Your Google profile visibility determines whether other players can see your gamer identity and game results through Google services. The app does not request your Google password, email inbox or friends list. Google Play Games may process usage and diagnostic information for its services. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage your gaming profile, sign-in choices and game data through Google Play Games or your Google Account.

Advertising and your choices

In the adult service path, the app uses Google AdMob for banner, full-screen interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Advertising services are not initialized for users under 18 or of unknown age. Google advertising services can process your IP-derived general location, ad/app identifiers, ad interactions and technical performance data for ad delivery, measurement and abuse prevention. They may share this information with advertising partners according to their settings and policies.

Where applicable, Google's consent messages ask for your choices. If Google requires a privacy-options control, you can reopen it from Settings → Ad Privacy. Android also provides advertising-ID controls. Buying Remove Ads suppresses banner and interstitial ads; you can still choose rewarded ads. These controls do not promise to disable every Google service or every technical request.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Purchases

In the adult service path, Remove Ads is a one-time Google Play in-app purchase. Purchase services are not initialized for users under 18 or of unknown age. Google Play handles payment. The app receives product ownership and transaction information so it can validate and acknowledge purchases and restore access. It also supplies a hashed account reference to Google Play. The app does not receive your credit card number or banking credentials. Google maintains payment records under its policies; clearing game progress does not cancel a purchase or request a refund.

Nearby multiplayer

Some games offer optional multiplayer with another paired Android device. Android Companion Device Manager presents the system device-selection and approval flow. With your Nearby devices permission, the app uses secure Bluetooth to connect to the approved paired device you select. The app does not request location access for discovery; Android may require its own Location Services setting for the system chooser. Device names and Bluetooth addresses are used for local device selection. Game state, moves, session identifiers and pause/restart messages are sent directly to that opponent. Google account credentials and your coin wallet are not sent to the opponent. There is no free-text chat or developer-operated multiplayer server.

Reminders and sharing

If enabled, game reminders use local device alarms and Android notifications. You can disable them in Settings or Android notification controls. They do not require a remote push account.

Sharing a game result generates an image and text on your device and opens Android's share chooser. Only the app you select receives the shared result. Its subsequent handling is governed by that app's policy. The app keeps temporary result images in its private cache until cleaned by the app or Android.

Retention, removal and support

Private device saves remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing local storage alone does not remove Google cloud saves, leaderboards or Google Play purchase records. Google cloud data follows Google's retention and deletion controls. To manage Google-hosted game data, follow Google's Play Games data-deletion instructions.

Before deleting Google-hosted progress, close GameBuddy on all devices and clear the app's local storage on every device that holds that profile's progress. Otherwise a remaining local copy may synchronize again when the app is reopened. In the audited version there is no integrated GameBuddy cloud-deletion button. If you need help with removing your data, email iiosturk@gmail.com. We cannot access your Google password or independently erase records controlled by Google. Google controls and any legal retention requirements remain applicable.

If you contact support, we use the contact details and information you voluntarily provide to respond. Do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to address the request and relevant legal obligations.

Security

The app uses Google's HTTPS/TLS services for online game and advertising data and secure paired Bluetooth for nearby matches. Local saves are kept in Android app-private storage. No security method guarantees protection in all circumstances.

Children and families

The app includes children and adults in its intended audience. The conservative service gate described above disables advertising and Google gaming services for everyone under 18 and for unknown ages. Local gameplay does not require a Google gaming account. Parents should manage device permissions and Google Play purchase authentication. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about a child's use or information, contact us at the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's features or services change. The date above identifies the current version.
