GameBuddy data removal

App: Game Buddy: Offline Games
Developer: emreozturk.dev
Android package: dev.emreozturk.gamebuddy
Updated: October 11, 2026

GameBuddy has no separate email/password account. Adult players can use Google's Play Games identity for cloud progress and leaderboards. Players under 18 or of unknown age keep progress locally and do not use these services.

Remove data stored on your device

In Android Settings, open Apps → GameBuddy → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device). This removes the app's private saves, settings, local backup/recovery copies and cached files on that device. Uninstalling also removes app-private data. If you use several devices, repeat this for each device that has the same player's saves.

Manage Google-hosted game data

Close GameBuddy on every device and clear those local copies before removing Google-hosted progress. A remaining copy can synchronize again when you reopen the app.

Use your Google Play Games profile to select Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data → Delete individual game data → GameBuddy. Google also offers these controls from Play Store Settings → General → Play Games Profile → Privacy & Settings. Follow Google's current instructions for your device. Only delete the entire Play Games profile if you intend to remove data for all games.

These are Google's deletion controls, not a GameBuddy server operated by the developer. Removing Google Play Games data may remove your game's achievements, progress and leaderboard records according to Google's service behavior. The app currently has no integrated snapshot-deletion button, and clearing a device alone does not delete cloud saves.

Request deletion of GameBuddy account or game data

You can begin a deletion request from GameBuddy Settings → Delete game data or directly from this page, without reinstalling the app. Email a GameBuddy data deletion request to iiosturk@gmail.com. State whether you want to remove local game progress, your Google-hosted GameBuddy progress and leaderboard data, or correspondence previously sent to support. We will explain the relevant removal steps and remove support correspondence we hold when applicable. GameBuddy has no separate developer-operated account; Google requires you to use your own Google profile controls for data it holds. We cannot access your Google password or independently erase Google-controlled game, payment or advertising records. Do not send passwords, card details or authentication codes.

What is retained

Google Play purchase/payment records are controlled by Google and are separate from game progress. Removing game data does not cancel your Remove Ads purchase or request a refund. Google may retain information under its policies and legal requirements. We may retain necessary support records for security, dispute handling or legal obligations, and will explain any applicable retention in responding to your request. We cannot independently erase Google's payment or advertising records.
